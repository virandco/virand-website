# VIRAND Brand & Visual Directives (Master Aesthetic)

## 1. Core Philosophy & Taglines
- Tagline: "BUILD PERCEPTION"
- Positioning: "Creative Systems" (Fusion of high-end cinematic art direction, automation, and digital strategy).
- Tone: Strategic, bold, authoritative, minimalist, cinematic. Zero AI slop, zero generic rounded pastel cards.

## 2. Color System (Strict Usage Ratios)
- Obsidian (#080808 / #000000) [60%]: Main absolute background.
- Pure White (#FFFFFF) [10%]: Primary high-contrast headlines and strong copy.
- Muted Grey / Off-White (#A1A1AA / #888888) [20%]: Body copy and secondary explanations.
- Wine Red (#6b0f1a) [5%]: Accent badges, key highlights, borders, and brand containers.
- Gold (#c9a84c) [5%]: Rare luxury accents, active tags, section counters, and high-priority CTAs.

## 3. Editorial Layout & Presentation Rules
- Massive Section Watermarks: Background numbers (01, 02, 03, 04) rendered in huge, muted typography behind section cards.
- Eyebrows & Tags: Every section begins with a small tracking-widest uppercase tag (e.g., `SISTEMAS`, `ALCANCE`, `METODOLOGÍA`).
- High-Contrast Typography:
  - Display / Headlines: Bold, uppercase sans-serif (League Spartan).
  - Body Text: Neutral, readable, clean sans-serif (Manrope).
- Asymmetric Grid & Minimal Containers: Dark borders (`border-white/10` or `border-[#6b0f1a]/30`), generous padding (`p-8` to `p-12`), subtle dark cards (`bg-[#0c0c0c]`).

## 4. Core Service Pillars
1. CONTENT SYSTEM: Atención, nutrición y conversión a través de contenido estratégico.
2. BRAND IDENTITY: Identidad de marca, diseño y sistemas visuales.
3. CREATIVE DIRECTION: Dirección de arte, posicionamiento y estética premium.
4. AI CONTENT: Generación de contenido con Inteligencia Artificial (exclusivamente creación de contenido).
