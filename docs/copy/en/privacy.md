# Privacy Policy

> Last updated: November 2026. Version 1.0.

## Controller

Andrés Virgües Fajardo, an individual and the owner of the VIRAND brand, is responsible for the data processing described here. VIRAND is a brand, not an incorporated company.

The contact channel for questions and exercising your rights is <a href="mailto:contacto@virand.co">contacto@virand.co</a>.

## Data we receive

When you submit the form, we receive your name, business type, brand status, services of interest, approximate budget, WhatsApp number or email, your message and the indication of consent. The form also sends the language and technical data to help prevent automated submissions.

The server uses the IP address and temporary request data to limit abuse. Submission limit counters are temporary and held in memory.

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive the contact details and content you choose to share in that conversation.

## Purpose and authorization

We use the data to handle your request, understand the project, respond through the contact channel you provided, schedule a call, follow up with you about our services, manage the commercial relationship and measure and improve the site (see “Site analytics”). Call booking is offered after form submission.

The form requires you to select the data processing authorization before submitting it. You may also contact <a href="mailto:contacto@virand.co">contacto@virand.co</a>.

## Form and providers

The form is sent to the site’s server route. After validation, the data and indication of consent are sent to Resend, an email provider in the US, which delivers the message to <a href="mailto:contacto@virand.co">contacto@virand.co</a>.

Vercel, a hosting provider in the US, hosts the site and runs the form route. At this stage, the site does not store requests in its own database or have a connected customer service agent.

The message remains in the contact inbox and is handled through that channel. Resend and Vercel are involved in transmission and hosting under their own services and policies.

## Site analytics

The site’s analytics service is Umami Cloud, operated by Umami Software, Inc. When enabled, the site measures visits, button clicks, questionnaire starts and completions, form submissions and calendar views as events. Only fixed categories are sent, without questionnaire answers, names, contact details or message content.

The site is not configured to install analytics cookies. According to <a href="https://umami.is/docs/faq">Umami’s documentation</a>, its tracker does not require cookies.

According to <a href="https://umami.is/privacy">Umami’s policy</a>, the provider briefly processes technical connection data, such as IP addresses, to deliver requests, calculate visitor identifiers and estimate approximate location. IP addresses are not stored as fields in standard analytics records. The same policy distinguishes those records from operational and security logs, which may include IP addresses.

The site respects the browser’s Do Not Track signal by disabling Umami tracking when the browser communicates that preference. This does not mean preventing all HTTP requests or providers’ technical logs.

## Theme preference and booking

The site’s own code stores only the light or dark theme preference in the browser, using localStorage under the virand-theme key. It does not write its own cookies or use sessionStorage.

After form submission, the booking option may display an embedded Google Calendar when enabled. When the calendar loads, Google may use its own cookies and process connection data. The site does not control those cookies. See <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google’s policies</a> and the <a href="/en/cookies/">site’s cookie policy</a>.

## Providers and visitors in other countries

Hosting and email delivery use the Vercel and Resend services described above, which are US providers. Umami Cloud is the analytics service described in this policy. These services may involve processing information outside Colombia or your country of residence.

If you visit from another country, your request is handled through the same flow. The providers’ policies explain their own processing: <a href="https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy">Resend</a>, <a href="https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy">Vercel</a> and <a href="https://umami.is/privacy">Umami</a>.

## Retention

We retain the data while it is needed to handle the request and the commercial relationship, and until the data subject requests deletion. If you request deletion, we check whether a legal obligation requires any information to be kept and explain what applies.

## Rights and contact

You may request access to, updates or corrections to your data, information about its use, proof of authorization, and deletion or withdrawal of consent where applicable under the law.

Write to <a href="mailto:contacto@virand.co">contacto@virand.co</a> and explain your request. If identity verification is needed to handle it, the necessary information will be requested through that channel, without publishing it on the site.

## Framework and changes

This policy refers to Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and its implementing regulations.

Changes to this text will be published on this page with their date and version.
